---
title: Why is life so competitive? I like losing.
date: 2026-05-15
math: true
---

I've been playing a lot of ping pong recently and a lot of uncles outright refuse to play table tennis / teach me unless I'm at a certain skill level. I wonder why we structure life so competitively. I think competition is the source of all my emotional pain. I grew up going to math competitions, competing for good grades in school, competing in fortnite battle royales to get the attention of girls. Watching my NBA dreams get crushed because I didn't choose giant in chracter selection when I spawned into this life.

Maybe I was raised in an environment where everyone I talked to was obsessed with strengths and ratings. Uncs won't play table tennis with me unless I'm at a certain strength level. For my own happiness, and the happiness of others, I want believe that true strength is kind.

The older people justify it by saying that the resources in this life are limited. We only have this many spots in this school, only this amount of select people can get this job.

I want to challenge that. All knowledge is on the internet. I learned all of my programming knowledge is on codeforces. if all of my knowledge is stolen from others, then WTF am I even competing for or on? They say you either win or you learn, and I think I like learning more than winning, what's the point of winning if you just make whoever lost sad?

I've decided that my purpose in this life is just to survive and be happy, I will gain knowledge and power if and only if it helps me survive. Curiously learning about the world gives me a sense of control. For example: I learn to cook when I'm by myself, but I don't need to cook at home. When I lose, I feel inadequate and that I need strengthen to live, so I learn. As I learn, I can create more beuatiful things. I don't think seeking to make myself better than others is the way I want to live my life.

Perhaps the goal in this life is self-expression, producing your own art or masterpiece. An expression of whatever you've built in your brain. My friends are sad that the LLMs are better than them at everything, and as LLMs get stronger, I don't think that competition between openAI and anthropic and big AI labs is the way to go, as we race to build technology, I think that this knowledge should be shared. The true driver of innovation is not competition and never was competition. I claim that the driver is pure curiousity. We ask questions and learn things, then learning things enables us to reach the frontier of knowledge and expand them. I dream of a less secretive, more friendly, more open world. I want to be able to ask anyone anything and get knowledge freely. I feel like the internet is already almost structured like this, so maybe we could model this in real life.
