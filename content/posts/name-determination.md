People go through life experiencing emotions, these can drive their actions. But what if your name determined your emotions? What if your name determined your life? What if you had no free will?

When you are born, you are given a name. This name may have the power to give you certain emotional characters which then drive your actions. For example, Michael Jordan is a name that has been associated with greatness.

[Michael Jordan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Jordan) is a name that has been associated with greatness. The name "Michael" is derived from the Hebrew name "Mikha'el," which means "who is like God?" The name "Jordan" is derived from the Hebrew word "Yarden," which means "to descend" or "to flow down."

The idea of free will is a philosophical question that has been debated for centuries. Some argue that our choices are predetermined by factors such as genetics, environment, and upbringing, while others believe that we have the ability to make independent decisions.

Now, consider the three individuals named Michael Jordan. Each of them has achieved success in their respective fields, but their paths to success have been different. This raises the question: is it possible that their names influenced their life choices and outcomes?

Life itself can be modeled as a simulation. True randomness is hard to achieve so algorithms can be used to simulate (https://www.wikiwand.com/en/Mersenne_Twister)[randomness]. This means that it is predictable, and that our lives are determined by a set of rules and algorithms. We can attempt to decompile reality to understand the rules and algorithms that govern our lives.

In this simulation, our names may serve as a guiding force, shaping our experiences and determining the trajectory of our lives.
